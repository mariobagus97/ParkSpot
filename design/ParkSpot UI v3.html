Idea: your parking photo is the album art. The app treats your car like the song that's playing: a "now parked" bar follows you everywhere, and Find is the Now Playing screen. #121212 base #181818 / #1f1f1f surfaces #1ed760 green (actions only) #ffa42b warning #f3727f negative

What changed from v2. Layout, colour and feel all follow Spotify now. There's a bottom tab bar (Home, Your spots). A "now parked" mini bar sits above it while a spot is active, like Spotify's now-playing bar, and tapping it opens Find. Find is laid out like the Now Playing screen: big square "album art" (your photo), title, a progress bar for the walk back, and a row of round controls with a green centre button.

Colour. Near-black surfaces (#121212, #181818, #1f1f1f, #252525). Green #1ed760 only on actions: Park, the shutter, Found it, Done, the active spot. Spotify takes colour from album art; ParkSpot takes it from your photo, so each Find screen gets its own gradient (here sample colours).

Type and shapes. Bold/regular contrast, compact sizes, UPPERCASE pill buttons with wide letter spacing, circles for icon controls, 6–8 px radius on photos and cards, heavy shadows on sheets and dialogs. Mockup uses DM Sans as the stand-in for Spotify's Circular.

Still Apple-standard underneath. Tab bar, sheet with grabber, swipe actions, system camera, MapKit, 44 pt+ targets, Dynamic Type (custom font registered with relativeTo: text styles), VoiceOver labels.

Gen-Z touches kept. Casual copy in both languages, filter chips (All / Malls / Streets) like Spotify's, recent spots as a shelf of cover-art tiles.

Sample data. Places, distances, times and photo colours are examples. Striped tiles stand in for your photos. Icons approximate SF Symbols.

Navigation (MVVM + C). AppCoordinator now owns a TabView (Home, Your spots) and the now-parked bar. Save coordinator: Park → Camera → Details sheet. Find coordinator: full-screen Find → Map. History coordinator under the second tab.

Not designed yet. App icon, location-denied screen (same pattern as 9), photo viewer, Settings (avatar menu).
